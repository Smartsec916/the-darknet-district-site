What it is for

ADMIN // BLACKBOOK helps you carry out and document authorised security assessments and site observations: write up what you see, attach the evidence, and produce one report. Use it only where you are authorised. You are responsible for recording and using information about other people lawfully.

  • Observation logs

    Date and time, assessment details (client, scope and authorisation reference), people, vehicles, location, notes and photos.

  • Technical and physical assessments

    Technical findings for networks, hosts and services, and physical-security findings for doors, badge readers, cameras and procedures.

  • Signal survey

    Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, cell, satellite and magnetic readings. A survey runs only when you start it and stops after 2 minutes. Each reading says whether the phone only listened or also transmitted. The app never changes your Wi-Fi, Bluetooth or location settings and never connects to a network or device.

  • Photo evidence and integrity

    Photos are kept as the original files, each with a SHA-256 fingerprint so you can show they have not changed.

  • Offline maps

    An overview map of the United States is built in. Add a detailed state map from a file for street-level detail.

  • Encrypted storage and backups

    The database and photos are encrypted on the device. Backups are protected by a password you choose, at least 10 characters.

  • Reports and export

    Export one HTML report with optional photos, and optionally mask device and network identifiers.

  • Locked by default

    PIN lock with optional fingerprint unlock. Screenshots and the recent-apps preview are blocked by default.
