Terms of the record

  • The Function is fictional. Its archives are not: anything published under Signal Intelligence is a real observation of a real radio signal.
  • No record is invented, filled in or simulated. Where nothing has been observed, the archive says so.
  • Automated analysis and unconfirmed identifications are labelled as such and kept apart from what was observed.
  • This terminal is read-only. It takes no submissions, comments or accounts.
