## The Temple's chant: `temple-chant.ogg` (18 Nov 2026; replaces the 15 Nov `temple.ogg`, now deleted)

| Item | Source | Licence | Commercial use |
|---|---|---|---|
| `temple-chant.ogg` (Zen Zephyr Temple; 288 s, stereo, Vorbis q1, 2.8 MB) | Commissioned by the owner and generated by Claude at his request with ElevenLabs Music (model v2.5) on the owner's paid ElevenLabs **Creator** plan, 18 Nov 2026. Prompt in brief: Tibetan-style overtone chanting (about 85% human, 15% synthetic), singing bowls, bells and low drones, about 50 BPM, meditative, for a cyberpunk AI monk's temple. Post-processing: a 12 s crossfade baked at the loop point (seamless loop), a low 852 Hz sine mixed in at -34 dB, loudness normalised to -20 LUFS. At the owner's request ("small digital undertones like digital rain") a quiet, high-passed digital-rain layer is mixed in (lowered a further 6 dB and its peaks evened out at his request the same day): a loop made with ElevenLabs Sound Effects (v3) on the same plan, 18 Nov 2026, also wrapped seamlessly at the same loop point. Nothing is generated at play time. | ElevenLabs terms for paid plans (Creator) | Covered by the owner's paid Creator plan per ElevenLabs' terms for paid plans; the plan and date are recorded here as the evidence available |

- The 27 Riffusion songs of the Sleeping Pod playlist are not here and were not touched.
- The prompts named films only as a description of mood. No existing recording, artist or melody was given to the generator as a reference.
- The full prompts and the generations are kept in the ElevenLabs flow "Darknet District - area soundtrack" on the owner's account.
- Cost: 900 credits per track, 12,600 for the 14.
