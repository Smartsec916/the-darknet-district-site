# Area soundtrack: where these recordings came from

| Item | Source | Licence | Commercial use |
|---|---|---|---|
| `*.ogg` (14 loops, one per area) | Generated once with ElevenLabs Music (model `eleven_music_v2_5`, instrumental, 60 seconds each, one take each) on David's ElevenLabs account, Starter plan, 15 Nov 2026. Each was levelled, trimmed of any quiet intro or ending, and folded into a loop (the last 3 seconds blended into the first 3), then stored here so the game never generates music at play time. | ElevenLabs terms for paid plans | David to confirm the Starter plan's commercial terms cover music used in the game; the plan and date are recorded here as the evidence available |
