Created with the built-in image generation tool for VOID//RUNNER. The portrait atlas is displayed as six individual portraits using CSS background positions; source artwork is preserved unchanged.

## characters.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: game character portrait atlas, a single image with exactly 3 columns and 2 rows of equally sized square portrait panels, no gutters, no borders, no text. Six distinctive adult space cyberpunk characters in consistent painterly cinematic game concept art, chest-up centered faces at the same scale, dark navy backgrounds, detailed expressive faces, subtle cyan and magenta rim lighting. Top left: Mara Voss, woman in her late 50s, short silver hair, warm tired eyes, olive work jacket, deceased employer's old friend. Top middle: Elias Ward, man in his 60s, greying beard, kind face, battered brown flight jacket, former employer shown in archival photo. Top right: Rook, lean male cargo broker in his 40s, dark undercut hair, amber cybernetic eye, charcoal coat. Bottom left: Iona Vale, Black woman in her 30s, purple braided hair, technical jacket, confident underground engineer. Bottom middle: Dr Sol, East Asian man in his 50s, glasses, teal medical workwear. Bottom right: Nyx, androgynous adult bartender, shaved sides, white hair, silver facial implants, dark vest. Each head must fit fully inside its own panel. Portrait artwork only, no interface, no labels, no writing. Landscape 3:2 atlas.

## bar.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: full-width background for a playable space-station bar in a cyberpunk game. Cinematic painterly concept art, widescreen 16:9. The Dead Channel bar aboard Meridian station: low worn metal counter and glowing bottle shelves on the left, shadowy seating booth with table on the right, central aisle for navigation, broad rear window looking out onto a docked battered space freighter and stars. Cyan and magenta neon tubes, warm amber table lamps, atmospheric soft haze, lived-in industrial details, intimate human scale. Empty of foreground characters because separate character portraits will be overlaid. Clear readable geography: bar counter left, booth right, exit hatch center rear. No text, no logos, no interface.
