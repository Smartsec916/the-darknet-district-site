## What remains for owner review

- The fight pit, District bar, VR area, arcade, NULL and smoking terrace use shared adult-venue policy. The historical record specifically attributes these limits to the owner. Consider individual venue access decisions if the current design calls for broader access. Milked also has separate adult performer and content rules. Published age and performer metadata continue to apply.
- Some legacy motion categories and the voice lookup use content-specific names and locked identities. A new motion class needs its source, real clip, rig, seat fit and age eligibility reviewed. Provider terms and asset licences govern any redistribution or repurposing.
- The registered street graph links the city quarters, Underground stairwells and the District door. Corporate District and Temple support placement and local movement. Their separate arrival scenes and taxi handoffs need registered NPC transitions and collision-tested arrivals before an escort can carry through. Offworld travel needs a destination adapter and a transport lifecycle. Scripted Milked reception is a separate scene without a general walked link. Player-scoped apartments, access phase, floor changes and newly authored rooms remain subject to the active room graph and runtime.
- Apartments require the skeletal indoor escort renderer. Cat travel into those rooms would need a procedural actor and indoor interaction adapter. Cat taxi travel would need boarding, carriage pose and unloading. Dax would need a movable actor outside the District and consistent UTC authority. The player-owned sleeping areas and locked staff rooms still require their existing access safeguards.
- A scheduled crossing walks toward the outgoing link and uses the registered entry in the active scene. Scenes are built around the local player. The schedule cannot simulate simultaneous actors across unloaded districts. Preserve current per-player escort state and keep the multiplayer identity, privacy and transport contracts intact.
