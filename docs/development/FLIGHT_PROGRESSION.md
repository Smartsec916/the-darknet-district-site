# Campaign flight progression

## Proposed rollout by route

The eight refit parts already have stable catalog IDs, prices, icons and fitted effects. Their general store listings are on hold. Existing purchases remain usable. The first Tessaly freight prototype and Mission 012’s safe Rook delivery work with the factory Kestrel.

**Recommended next fully authored area:** expand the existing Meridian arrival into a compact Rook freight district around the hangar, his Dead Channel contact point and the Outfitter. Mission 012 already brings players there. Give the player a clear route from the dock to Rook and their first optional repeat job, one local story lead and a readable repair or equipment comparison. Then test a stock Kestrel against one rookie on an optional job before introducing two trained pilots or placing any part into stock. Tessaly Belt Anchorage is the follow-up for a physical Function inspection office and repair counter. This is a progression proposal for owner review.

The first approach to Rook is safe. Campaign jobs keep authored objectives and can have their own enemy counts. Rook’s routine freight starts with one opponent in the opening local lanes and scales with completed runs and destination. Local transit rolls are stable for a route and campaign checkpoint. Interstellar hops retain their existing travel rules. Fighting clears the encounter. Holding Warp can escape a standard interdiction, and damage interrupts the spool.

Enemy weapons gain lead and a tighter spread by skill. Interceptors gain 2 armor and gunships gain 10 armor, harder pulse hits and missiles. Authored ships can override those values. The ship and loadout calculations are shared between campaign and Flight Skirmish, with each skirmish’s difficulty still chosen in setup.

Cipher shares the Kestrel’s workboat airframe, framed canopy, swept wings, red service markings, landing struts, and exposed twin engines. Its finish adds a cooler ceramic coat, machined exhaust collars, and a flight registration plate. The painted cockpit shares the same framing and instruments with a Cipher-labeled display and distinct instrument trim. Horizon is planned to use the same performance and refits as Cipher with a 6-unit cargo hold. Existing Spectre entitlements retain their own hull.
