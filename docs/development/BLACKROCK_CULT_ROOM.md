# The Blackrock Cult Room (19 Nov 2026)

Branch `claude/blackrock-cult`, build `2026-11-19-blackrock-cult-1`. Pushed, no PR, not merged.

The owner's queued request (AI_HANDOFF.md, "The Blackrock Cult Room, for one mission"): one large square basement room in a corporate building, two storeys tall, one door centred on one side, the owl stencilled on the far wall with an altar and a small box under it, seven figures in black hooded robes, an initiate who draws a paper card out of the box. **The player never goes there:** it is seen only through a hacked security camera in a top corner, recorded to the V1 Gallery as a video.

## What the owner gets (plain words)

- **In a mission:** add a step **Hack the access point** (any access point the editor already offers: a street door, an apartment or a location's door) and choose **Camera feed** on it. When the player finishes the hack, the screen switches to the cult room's camera: top corner, looking down, the V1 Camera App look (corner brackets, timecode, zoom on the mouse wheel) with a red REC light. The player stays where they are, safe: their world is paused (nothing moves, nothing can reach them) and their keys do nothing.
- **Recording is automatic.** It starts as the feed comes up (the initiate already kneeling, just before the six enter) and stops by itself when everyone has left and the door has shut. The video (WebM) is saved in the **V1 Gallery** (it plays there; this browser only, never uploaded) and the view returns to the player by itself. **The step completes when the recording is saved.** If the Gallery is full the scene still plays, the step waits, and the player is told to free space and hack the camera again.
- **The choreography** (the owner's, 19 Nov 2026), all of it slow and practised: the initiate kneels at the centre facing the owl; the door opens; the six enter in one line through the door and walk round him until they stand evenly spaced, 60 degrees apart (they reach their places at the same moment); a pause, then they turn together to face him; the door shuts; the six speak to him and he answers (every line is an empty slot for the owner's words: empty means silence, nothing shown); he rises; the six turn together to face the owl; he walks slowly to the altar, opens the box and takes out the paper card, reads it, reacts in shock and exclaims (the owner's line), the six laugh a little; the door opens; all seven walk out in a line, the initiate first; the door shuts; the recording stops. About three minutes.
- **Edit it in the Mission Editor:** Add a location > **Other** > **Blackrock cult room (camera feed only)** adds the feed to the mission (it is not walkable and joins no module). Its card (Form view, under the mission's locations) and **EDIT THE SCENE** under the hack step (Form and Blocks views) show the timeline: every beat in order with the pause before it; who, what they turn to face, where they walk, the walking speed (scene-wide or per beat), the circle's size, the gap in the exit line, animations; each **line** has a words box and **IMPORT VOICE FILE** (the same as every other line). + ADD A BEAT, ↑ ↓, ×, RESET TO THE OWNER'S CHOREOGRAPHY, **▶ PREVIEW THE SCENE** (plays the feed in the game without recording), the length and any problems in plain words. Or press **+ ADD THE BLACKROCK CULT ROOM FEED** on the hack step itself.

## The room

`void-runner/cult-room-build.js`, its own Babylon scene on the game's engine, built only while a feed shows it and disposed afterwards (the player's world is untouched).

- 14 m square, 7 m high. Board-formed concrete walls (form lines, tie holes, stains from the ring beam), pilasters on the side walls, a ring beam, an exposed ceiling (two deep beams, a ventilation duct with hangers, a pipe run along the west wall, a cable tray), a stained slab with a drain under the initiate, conduit and junction boxes, a fire panel, an exit sign and a caged bulkhead light over the door.
- **The owl:** the owner's own art (`void-runner/art/blackrock/owl-stencil.webp`, 1024 x 683) made into a painted stencil: only the coloured shapes are paint (the backdrop and the black linework between the shapes are keyed out with a soft alpha, so the bare concrete shows through them; colours bled under the edges so there is no dark halo), a faint overspray mist round the shapes, the concrete's grain through the paint, blotchy paint loss, flecks and drips. 8.4 x 5.6 m on the far wall, lit by two grazing wall-washers from the ceiling (a soft fall-off down the wall, no pool of light with an edge).
- **The altar:** a black stone block on a plinth, a black cloth runner, ten candles (a flickering warm light), a lacquered box with a brass catch whose lid opens; the paper card goes from the box into the initiate's right hand.
- **The door and the stub corridor:** a steel sliding door centred on the near wall; behind it 4.2 m of dim service corridor (concrete, a pipe, a caged warm wall light), only so the open door never shows empty space.
- **Light** (6 of the 8 allowed): the owl wash, a cool pool over the circle, the candles, the door lamp, the corridor lamp, a low fill. Fog, vignette and a muted, cold colour grade (a security camera's look); the recording adds scanlines, grain and the camera's burn-in.
- **The overlay** (the owner: "put the reticle on the camera view just like the camera"): the V1 Camera App's own frame (corner brackets, edge ticks) and centre aperture with its crosshair, same markup as `device-camera.js`, styled by the Camera App's rules copied at run time from `device-v1.css`; no battery reading (a wired security camera; the owner). On screen only: like the Camera App's photos, the recording does not carry it (the recording has its own burn-in: camera name, date and time, REC).
- **The camera:** in the top corner over the door side (`cult-room.js ROOM.camera`), seeing the door, the circle, the altar and the owl; zoom 1x, 1.5x, 2x, 3x.

## The robed figure

`void-runner/assets/models/crowd/npc-blackrock-robe.glb` (3.0 MB, 30,485 triangles, 24 joints, JPEG maps). A NEW figure, not a recoloured monk: a full-length black hooded robe of heavy wool, a deep pointed hood with the face entirely in shadow (no features from any angle), long sleeves over the hands (the hands' skin-coloured texels were painted to the cloth's black), a braided cord belt with tassels. Meshy (text-to-image concept, image-to-3D, remesh 30k) on the owner's account: **44 credits** (balance 140 to 96; ledger in `MESHY_CHARACTERS.md`). **Meshy's auto-rig refused it** ("could not be rigged": the robe hides the legs; no credits were charged), so it was rigged on the owner's PC in Blender (background process): the 24-joint Meshy skeleton taken from `npc-monk-tech`'s rig and scaled to a 1.73 m figure under the hood, the weights transferred from that monk's skinned robe (bone heat could not solve the closed robe), smoothed, at most four per vertex. Spec `tools/animation-pipeline/characters/npc-blackrock-robe.json`.

- **Clips** (retargeted locally from the Rokoko free mocap set, free): `walk` (the game's street walk, played at speed / 1.2 m/s so the feet keep to the floor: the processional walk is 0.5 m/s, the initiate's 0.42), `kneel` (on one knee, Rise_01 2-9 s), `rise` (Rise_01 10.3-13.3 s, once), `stand` (Rise_01 15-21 s: standing completely still, arms down; no idle variation), `read` (StarWars_TrooperCheckingPapers 2-8 s: a paper held at chest height), `shock` (DoubleTakes 25.5-30.5 s, once), `laugh`, `talk`, `idle`. The ranges were chosen from hip, knee, head and hand heights measured in Blender.
- **Seven instances**: heights 0.96 to 1.05 and the hood a little wider or narrower (the head joint's width); the initiate wears the same robe (the owner may want a detail on it later).

## The timeline (for Claude and Codex)

`void-runner/cult-room.js` (pure, Node-tested). Data: `mission.feeds = [{id, room:'blackrock-cult', scene:{radius?, speed?, beats:[...]}}]`; a hack step `{kind:'hack', ap, feed:'<feed id>'}` (it opens no door unless `unlock:true`). Beats, played in order, each after `wait` seconds: `pause {secs}`, `door {open}`, `enter {speed?}`, `turn {who: six|initiate|robed-N, to: initiate|owl|door|centre, secs?}`, `line {who: initiate|robed-1..6|robed-all, text, anim?, secs?}`, `rise`, `walk {who, to: altar|centre|spot, speed?}`, `card`, `anim {who, clip, secs?}`, `exit {speed?, gap?}`. Order rules (sceneProblems, repeated by the server's `feed_scene_problems`): the six enter once, speak and turn only while in the room; the initiate rises once, walks only standing, takes the card only at the altar; everyone leaves together; after the exit only pauses and the door.

- **Entry maths:** the six walk one path (in through the door, north to the circle, round it) at one speed, spaced exactly one sixth of the circle (R x 60 degrees), so when the leader reaches the last place each walker is on its own place at the same moment, evenly spaced. `compile()` turns beats into a track per figure (hold, turn with an eased curve, path, once, loop) and events (door, lines, box, card, exit order); `poseAt(t)` gives the place, heading, clip and clip time; the build sets the clip frame from the timeline time (no drift; tests run it faster).
- **Exit maths:** the initiate turns and walks to the door and out; each of the six joins behind him in order of where its place meets his path (north first; west before east at the same depth), stepping sideways onto the path just as its place in the line arrives (gap 1.6 m by default).
- **Mission event:** `camera-feed.js` wraps `VoidLiveContent.hack`: a job with a feed plays it instead of completing the step; the saved recording calls `VoidLiveContent.feedSaved(job)`, the event `{type:'feed', ap, feed, mission}` that the step matches (`mission-content.js`).
- **The player is paused:** `camera-feed.js` wraps game.js `update` (not run while a feed shows) and `draw` (the feed's scene is rendered and drawn through the `#space` canvas); every other element on screen is hidden and restored after.
- **Recording:** a 960 x 540 canvas (the game's picture plus the burn-in: CAM B2-07, the in-game date and time, REC, the words of a line when there are any), `captureStream(24)`, MediaRecorder (VP9, VP8 or WebM), the bitrate set from the scene's length (0.45 to 1.6 Mbit/s) so a file stays under the Gallery's 48 MB cap. A poster frame is taken at the first line. `device-gallery.js addVideo`: kind `video`, 5% of the device's storage, at most 48 MB and 10 minutes; never posted to News; not offered when attaching a photo to a message. Voice: a line with an imported (or recorded) voice for its exact words plays through `voice-clips.js` (the recording itself is silent).
- **Server:** `void_runner_live_content.py feed_problems` / `feed_scene_problems` / `feed_shape_problems` (mission key `feeds`, step key `feed`), `void_runner_drafts.py` (the editor's server copy), admin inventory `feeds`. Nothing new in the save (`save-manifest.json` unchanged): the step's state is the usual objective flag.

## Not done / weak

- Lines are empty until the owner writes them; with no voice imported the scene is silent. The recording carries no sound (only the picture).
- The hood's mouth and hands: the hands are painted black and stay inside the long sleeves in most poses, but a raised hand (`shock`) can show a dark hand shape. The robe's skirt follows the legs (weights from the monk robe): at 0.5 m/s it reads as heavy cloth, at speed it would look like trousers.
- Turns in place use the still `stand` pose rotated slowly (the robe hides the feet; no stepping clip). The kneel is on one knee (the mocap's).
- Not verified: real hardware and frame rate on the ROG Ally, VR, the recording's size on a real GPU (the software-GL test machine renders slowly), Edge's MediaRecorder codecs.
