# The Darknet District development roadmap

This is the short **current-scope index**, not a replacement for canon or asset audits. The owner's latest Darknet District instructions supersede older start-location and active-mission descriptions in `VOID_RUNNER_HANDOFF.md` and `CODEX_HANDOFF.md`. Historical mission/story decisions stay archived for future reuse; new canon still follows the approval stages in `CLAUDE.md` and `VOID_RUNNER_HANDOFF.md` §11.

## Approved direction and order

1. **First playable website milestone:** Desktop players start and resume in Sleeping Pod 01, keep their saves/progress, can use music and lighting and explore the District/NeoChinatown. Old missions stay inactive, not deleted. Keep the unlisted route, phone gate, regular mobile site, and homepage ENTER unpublished until approval.
2. **Graphics in scope:** Improve pod 01, the main District interior, shared hallways, and common areas using the Tesana ZIP audit where rights, engine compatibility, animation, and low/high performance are verified. Keep the other nine pods for later. Do not replace a named NPC or import Tesana characters without proven animation compatibility and rights.
3. **Social presentation:** One visible profile style for players and NPCs; one floating, non-blocking message style for NPC, companion, and future player conversations. Preserve proximity for starting physical interactions; keep conversations readable while moving away. Reuse Social for history, filters, privacy, blocking/reporting plans, and later mobile access. Real account/NPC permissions remain separate on the server.
4. **Characters and Admin:** One persistent character per secured account; simple selection from compatible assets first, more appearance options only when they render correctly. The administrator eventually controls the existing Admin character in a shared room; a server-owned lease must remove the NPC, transfer his duties to Iris, and restore the NPC safely on disconnect without duplicates. This is approved design, **not implemented multiplayer behavior**.
5. **Achievements and tools:** Shared website/arcade/District/future-mission definitions with reusable verified events, categories (merchandise, District, arcade, community), progress, profile display, and public merchandise priority. Administrator-only manager eventually edits definitions/icons and audits grants/revocations. Client claims cannot verify purchases, scores, or missions; Shopify and production databases stay untouched without approval.
6. **Multiplayer after the playable foundation:** Social rooms only—identity, presence, appearance, position/animation, friends, messaging, invitations, and shared visibility. Spaceflight, combat, and mission progress remain single-player. Reuse existing identity and economical infrastructure; do not claim multiplayer works before two independent clients interact in one room. See the plan on PR #191 (`docs/void-runner/social-universe-architecture.md` on that branch) and `SOCIAL_FOUNDATION.md`; the plan's recommendations are not automatic approvals. **Status 2026-11-05:** a **local-only** two-player prototype works (dev hub in `tools/district-hub-dev/`, client `void-runner/remote-players.js`, emulators only): two browsers see each other's server-assigned character, movement, facing and walk/idle in the District interior at Low and High (`MULTIPLAYER_PROTOTYPE.md`). It is **not** public multiplayer: no hosting, no chat, no trading, no street rooms. The infrastructure audit and the smallest-safe path to two real players are in `FIREBASE_MULTIPLAYER_AUDIT.md`; every step that creates a service or publishes needs owner approval.

## Protected scope and standing decisions

No graphics or physical-behavior changes in the VR Play area, nightclub, stores/separate interiors, NeoChinatown exterior, Corporate District, other planets/stations, or spaceflight areas. VR companion **dialogue UI** is in scope; their models, animations, movement, personalities, relationships, and follow behavior stay. Keep Iris off the regular website Sleeping Pod page and out of the 3D game as a **website chatbot**; the established **in-game** Iris NPC remains. Keep hosted music shared, personal files browser-local, Share Links retired, and costs minimal. Do not change production, merge `main`, or publish without owner approval.

## Open decisions and gates

- **Before any live test or deployment:** owner approval, backup of the live browser save, Render game-API Auto-Deploy branch check, 2025 Riffusion/Producer.ai music rights, Tesana asset rights, and physical PC/ROG Ally low/high graphics review. Follow `FIRST_LIVE_TEST_RUNBOOK.md` for deployment and rollback. A merge to `main` publishes GitHub Pages.
- **Pending administrator action (recorded, not requested during current milestones):** read the deployed Firestore ruleset and confirm no broad allow overrides the repository's deny-all fragments; confirm the Firebase plan, Auth providers, App Check and the Render API settings (`FIREBASE_MULTIPLAYER_AUDIT.md` section 10).
- **Before social launch:** age policy, chat/privacy defaults, moderation capacity, retention, hosting/cost, and production data model. No paid service or public chat activation is approved. The revised hallway floor plan and pod allocation are open; do not invent them.
- **Before asset or music publishing tools:** verify per-asset licenses, animation retargeting, and rights for any Tesana shared-library model; settle the Music Manager's server credential/storage and upload rules (`MUSIC_MANAGER_DECISION.md`). Personal music must never be uploaded.
- **Story/world decisions:** use `VOID_RUNNER_HANDOFF.md` §11 and David's design-source documents. The older mission content remains inactive; proposed planets, missions, names, and dialogue are not made canon by appearing in a planning document.
