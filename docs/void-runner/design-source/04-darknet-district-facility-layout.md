# THE DARKNET DISTRICT
## Official Facility Layout — VOID//RUNNER
**Location:** NeoChinatown, Tessaly System

## 1. General Overview

The Darknet District is a secure, technologically advanced underground establishment located within the dense cyberpunk urban environment of NeoChinatown.

The facility combines underground commerce, entertainment, cybersecurity, advanced technology, temporary accommodations, and private meeting spaces.

It should feel like a functioning establishment with regular visitors, employees, merchants, and security personnel.

The interior has an industrial cyberpunk aesthetic featuring dark metals, exposed machinery, electronic displays, neon accents, security equipment, and holographic advertisements.

The facility is designed around a central hallway connecting its different rooms and services.

Players should be able to explore the interior naturally without unnecessary loading screens.

---

# 2. Exterior — NeoChinatown Alley

The player approaches The Darknet District through a narrow alley in NeoChinatown.

The surrounding neighborhood is densely developed, with towering buildings, neon advertisements, industrial equipment, and crowded streets.

### Environmental details

- Neon signs with Chinese and English lettering.
- Holographic advertisements.
- Industrial ventilation equipment.
- Exposed electrical cables and pipes.
- Security cameras.
- Utility doors and service entrances.
- Atmospheric lighting and environmental sounds.
- Occasional pedestrians and NPCs.

The entrance to The Darknet District is deliberately understated.

A reinforced security door provides access to the facility.

The exterior should suggest that something technologically sophisticated exists behind an otherwise unremarkable entrance.

---

# 3. Security Vestibule

Immediately inside the entrance is a secured vestibule.

This is a small transitional room separating the public alley from the private facility.

### Features

- Reinforced exterior door.
- Identification or badge scanner.
- Security cameras.
- Electronic access terminal.
- Interior security door.
- Security status displays.

The player enters the vestibule, completes the necessary access procedure, and passes through the interior door.

The process should be immersive but quick.

Security systems should remain consistent with the organization's emphasis on privacy and controlled access.

---

# 4. Main Hallway

After passing through security, the player enters the main hallway.

**This hallway is the central spine of The Darknet District.**

All major public areas connect to it directly or through short adjoining passages.

### Visual design

- Dark metallic walls.
- Industrial flooring.
- Exposed structural beams.
- Overhead lighting.
- Neon and LED accents.
- Holographic advertisements.
- Directional signage.
- Security cameras.
- Wall-mounted displays.
- Ambient machinery.
- Occasional NPCs walking between rooms.

The hallway should be wide enough for natural player and NPC movement.

Players should be able to identify important rooms using visible signs rather than relying entirely on HUD markers.

---

# 5. VR and Arcade Area

A dedicated entertainment area branches off the main hallway.

This space contains virtual reality equipment, arcade machines, and futuristic gaming systems.

### Interior features

- VR stations.
- Arcade cabinets.
- Interactive screens.
- Neon lighting.
- Seating.
- Gaming advertisements.
- NPC players and spectators.

This area should be visually energetic and noticeably different from the quieter residential sections.

### Gameplay opportunities

Future activities may include:

- Arcade minigames.
- Flight simulators.
- Shooting simulations.
- Competitive challenges.
- Optional missions.

These are expansion possibilities, not automatically approved features.

---

# 6. Bar and Lounge

The bar is a social gathering area accessible from the main hallway.

It provides a place for visitors to relax, socialize, and exchange information.

### Interior features

- Main bar counter.
- Bartender.
- Tables and chairs.
- Booth seating.
- Ambient lighting.
- Music.
- NPC patrons.
- Digital menus and advertisements.

The atmosphere should be somewhat darker and more relaxed than the arcade.

### Gameplay opportunities

The bar can support:

- Conversations with NPCs.
- Mission introductions.
- Rumors.
- Information brokers.
- Returning characters.
- Optional side missions.

NPCs should have natural idle animations, including drinking, talking, and sitting.

---

# 7. Admin and Iris's Shop

The shop operated by Admin and Iris is one of the most important locations in the facility.

It specializes in advanced technology, electronics, equipment, and unusual merchandise.

### Interior features

- Service counter.
- Equipment displays.
- Shelving.
- Digital product catalogs.
- Holographic product advertisements.
- Specialized electronic devices.
- Secure storage.
- Admin's workstation.
- Iris's interaction position.

### Admin

Admin is a central authority associated with The Darknet District.

He may provide specialized equipment, information, and missions.

### Iris

Iris is an advanced female android and the organization's Chief Security Officer.

She assists with security, customer interactions, and potentially specialized mission assignments.

### Gameplay functionality

The shop should support:

- Buying equipment.
- Selling appropriate items.
- NPC conversations.
- Mission interactions.
- Specialized upgrades.
- Future story progression.

The shop should remain a recurring destination throughout the campaign.

---

# 8. Vending Machine Area

A small vending area is positioned along the main hallway or within a recessed alcove.

### Features

- Food vending machines.
- Drink vending machines.
- Illuminated product displays.
- Small standing or seating area.
- Trash receptacles.
- Environmental details.

The vending machines help make the District feel like an operational facility rather than a series of mission rooms.

Optional purchasing interactions can be introduced later.

---

# 9. Sleeping Pod Section

A quieter section of the facility contains sleeping accommodations.

The sleeping pods provide temporary lodging for travelers and visitors.

### Interior features

- Individual sleeping pods.
- Pod identification numbers.
- Privacy doors or screens.
- Personal storage.
- Dim lighting.
- Access terminals.
- Ventilation equipment.

The sleeping area should have a quieter atmosphere than the arcade or bar.

### Gameplay functionality

Sleeping pods may support:

- Resting.
- Saving progress.
- Temporary accommodation.
- Story-related events.

The exact functionality should be coordinated with the game's existing save system.

---

# 10. Private Meeting Room

A meeting room provides a secure location for private conversations.

### Interior features

- Conference table.
- Chairs.
- Wall-mounted displays.
- Communications terminals.
- Privacy-oriented lighting.
- Electronic access controls.

### Gameplay opportunities

This room may be used for:

- Mission briefings.
- Private negotiations.
- Sensitive conversations.
- Meetings with Admin.
- Faction-related encounters.
- Important story decisions.

The meeting room should feel functional and private.

---

# 11. Staircase and Smoking Area

A staircase connects the main facility to a separate smoking area.

The smoking area is a more informal space where visitors can step away from the main activity.

### Features

- Industrial staircase.
- Ventilation equipment.
- Seating.
- Ash receptacles.
- Dim lighting.
- Environmental details.
- Occasional NPCs.

The area may eventually contain informal conversations or optional encounters.

# 13. NPC Population and Atmosphere

The Darknet District should feel populated but not overcrowded.

Potential NPC types include:

- Shop customers.
- Arcade players.
- Bar patrons.
- Technicians.
- Security personnel.
- Travelers.
- Merchants.
- Information brokers.
- Maintenance workers.

NPCs should have appropriate animations and behaviors.

Some may walk between areas, while others remain at workstations or seating locations.

The facility should feel active even when the player is not participating in a mission.

---

# 14. Player Navigation

The player's intended arrival sequence is:

1. Arrive in NeoChinatown.
2. Locate The Darknet District entrance.
3. Enter the security vestibule.
4. Complete the access procedure.
5. Enter the main hallway.
6. Explore the available rooms.
7. Interact with Admin, Iris, and other NPCs.
8. Access shops, entertainment, accommodations, and missions.
9. Return to the exterior when ready to leave.

Navigation should be intuitive.

Use physical signage, lighting, and environmental landmarks.

Avoid excessive floating markers where the environment itself can guide the player.

---

# 15. Rules for Future Development

1. The Darknet District is located in NeoChinatown, Tessaly System.
2. Preserve its industrial cyberpunk atmosphere.
3. Maintain the security vestibule and central hallway.
4. Preserve all established functional areas.
5. Keep Admin and Iris as important recurring characters.
6. Do not redesign or relocate existing rooms without approval.
7. Allow future missions to reuse established locations.
8. Keep NPC movement and collision systems functional.
9. Optimize the environment for browser performance.
10. Verify existing geometry before implementing layout changes.

## Final Design Objective

The Darknet District should feel like a believable underground technological community.

It is a place where travelers, independent operators, hackers, merchants, and unusual individuals cross paths.

Its physical design should support exploration, commerce, social interaction, and future mission development.

The facility should become a recognizable recurring destination within VOID//RUNNER, not merely a one-time campaign location.
