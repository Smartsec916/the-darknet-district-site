# VOID//RUNNER — Admin Introduces the Communion and Natural Order

**Location:** The Darknet District, NeoChinatown, Tessaly System  
**NPC:** Admin  
**Mission Type:** Main Campaign — Faction Introduction

---

**ADMIN:**
"Ah, you must be the pilot Rook sent. He mentioned you might stop by. Says you're reliable enough. That's usually all I need to hear."

**PLAYER:**
"Rook said you might have some work."

**ADMIN:**
"Always got work. Just depends on where you're willing to go."

*(Admin glances at his terminal.)*

**ADMIN:**
"Things have been getting a little interesting out there lately. The Communion and the Natural Order are at each other's throats again."

**PLAYER:**
"Who are they?"

**ADMIN:**
"Communion's big on artificial intelligence. Practically worship the stuff. Natural Order? They'd rather smash a computer than let one make a decision for them."

*(He shrugs.)*

**ADMIN:**
"Been arguing about it for generations. Occasionally they shoot at each other. Usually somewhere inconvenient for the rest of us."

**PLAYER:**
"Sounds like trouble."

**ADMIN:**
"Eh. Trouble's good for business. Long as it stays somewhere else."

*(Admin brings up two shipping contracts.)*

**ADMIN:**
"Anyway, I've got two deliveries that need moving. One's headed into Communion territory, the other's going to a Natural Order outpost. Both pay decent."

**PLAYER:**
"What's the cargo?"

**ADMIN:**
"Equipment. Supplies. Nothing particularly exciting."

*(He pauses.)*

**ADMIN:**
"Well, nothing that's supposed to be exciting."

**ADMIN:**
"So, what do you think? Which direction you feel like heading?"

---

## PLAYER DIALOGUE OPTIONS

### Option A — Ask About the Communion

**PLAYER:**
"Tell me about the Communion delivery."

**ADMIN:**
"Advanced electronics. Research equipment, mostly. They're always building something over there. Pays well enough."

*Return to the dialogue choices.*

### Option B — Ask About the Natural Order

**PLAYER:**
"What about the Natural Order?"

**ADMIN:**
"Mechanical components. Industrial supplies. They prefer things that don't think for themselves. Can't say I blame them sometimes."

*Return to the dialogue choices.*

---

## FINAL PLAYER DECISION

**1. "I'll take the Communion delivery."**

- Accept the Communion cargo mission.
- Add the mission to the Mission Log.
- Track the Communion delivery destination.
- Begin the journey into Communion territory.

**2. "I'll take the Natural Order delivery."**

- Accept the Natural Order cargo mission.
- Add the mission to the Mission Log.
- Track the Natural Order delivery destination.
- Begin the journey into Natural Order territory.

**3. "I'll think about it."**

- End the conversation.
- Accept neither mission.
- Both contracts remain available when the player returns to Admin.

---

## MISSION DESIGN NOTES FOR CLAUDE / CODEX

**This is the player's first introduction to the Communion and Natural Order conflict.**

Admin should remain casual, pragmatic, and relatively indifferent to their ideological differences.

The player should not yet know which faction is right or wrong.

The purpose of these cargo missions is to introduce the player to the factions through firsthand experience.

### Important rules

- Choosing a delivery does not establish faction allegiance.
- Both factions should remain accessible later.
- The player should be able to ask about both deliveries before deciding.
- Asking about a delivery must not automatically accept it.
- Only the player's explicit final selection activates a cargo mission.
- Do not automatically start the unselected mission.
- The player can decline and return later.
- The chosen mission should automatically become the tracked mission.
- The mission destination should appear in the navigation system.
- Do not reveal major faction secrets during this introductory conversation.

### Next Story Development

The Communion and Natural Order destinations, NPCs, delivery events, and subsequent mission chains must be presented as separate proposals for approval before becoming official campaign content.
