# VOID//RUNNER — RETURN TO THE DARKNET DISTRICT
## Mission Completion, Payment & Iris's Second Cargo Offer

**Location:** The Darknet District, NeoChinatown, Tessaly System  
**NPC:** Iris — Chief Security Officer  
**Mission Type:** Main Campaign — Faction Introduction, Part 2

---

## 1. RETURN FOR PAYMENT

After successfully delivering the cargo to either the Communion or the Natural Order, the player's mission objective updates.

**MISSION OBJECTIVE:**
"Return to The Darknet District and collect payment."

The delivery is recorded as successful, but the mission remains active until the player returns and receives payment.

The navigation marker directs the player back to The Darknet District in NeoChinatown.

Upon entering Admin's shop, the player discovers that Admin is no longer behind the counter.

Instead, Iris is handling business.

---

# 2. IRIS — FIRST RETURN CONVERSATION

*(Iris notices the player approaching the counter.)*

**IRIS:**
"Ah, Rook's pilot. Back in one piece, I see. That's encouraging."

**PLAYER:**
"Where's Admin?"

**IRIS:**
"Occupied. I'm handling things for now. Don't worry, he left instructions to pay you."

*(Iris checks the delivery records.)*

**IRIS:**
"Let's see... cargo received, delivery confirmed, and no complaints from the recipient. How refreshingly uneventful."

*(The player's payment is transferred.)*

**IRIS:**
"There you are. Payment received. Pleasure doing business with you."

**MISSION COMPLETE**

- Award the agreed credits.
- Mark the original cargo mission completed.
- Record which faction received the delivery.
- Update the player's Mission Log.
- Unlock Iris's second cargo offer.

---

# 3. IRIS OFFERS THE REMAINING DELIVERY

*(As the player prepares to leave, Iris speaks again.)*

**IRIS:**
"Oh, before you go. Admin had another shipment lined up. The one you didn't take."

**PLAYER:**
"Still available?"

**IRIS:**
"Apparently. Nobody's been particularly eager to collect it."

*(Iris brings up the remaining shipping contract.)*

## IF THE PLAYER FIRST DELIVERED TO THE COMMUNION

**IRIS:**
"This one's headed to the Natural Order. Mechanical components, industrial supplies. Nothing terribly sophisticated."

*(She pauses briefly.)*

**IRIS:**
"At least, not by my standards."

## IF THE PLAYER FIRST DELIVERED TO THE NATURAL ORDER

**IRIS:**
"This shipment's for the Communion. Electronics and research equipment. They do seem to have an endless appetite for technology."

*(She glances at the cargo manifest.)*

**IRIS:**
"Some of it is actually rather interesting."

---

# 4. PLAYER DECISION

**PLAYER OPTION A:**
"I'll take the delivery."

**IRIS:**
"Excellent. I've transferred the shipping details to your mission log. Try not to lose anything expensive."

**GAMEPLAY ACTIONS:**

- Accept the remaining faction delivery.
- Add the mission to the Mission Log.
- Assign the appropriate cargo.
- Update the navigation destination.
- Track the new mission.

**PLAYER OPTION B:**
"Not right now."

**IRIS:**
"Of course. I'll keep the contract available. For now."

**GAMEPLAY ACTIONS:**

- Do not accept the second delivery.
- Keep the mission available through Iris.
- Allow the player to leave and return later.

---

# 5. SECOND DELIVERY COMPLETION

After completing the second faction delivery, the player must once again return to The Darknet District for payment.

Iris remains the payment contact.

## RETURN CONVERSATION

**IRIS:**
"Back again. You're becoming something of a regular."

**PLAYER:**
"Delivery's finished."

**IRIS:**
"So I see. Both shipments delivered, both clients satisfied. Remarkable, considering they can barely tolerate each other's existence."

*(Iris transfers the payment.)*

**IRIS:**
"There you go. All settled."

*(She pauses.)*

**IRIS:**
"Funny thing about those two. They disagree about practically everything, but somehow they both manage to keep us in business."

**MISSION COMPLETE**

- Award payment.
- Mark the second cargo mission completed.
- Record that the player has visited both faction destinations.
- Preserve access to both factions.
- Unlock the next approved campaign chapter when available.

---

# 6. IMPORTANT GAMEPLAY RULES

## Payment

Cargo deliveries require two separate steps:

1. Deliver the shipment to the destination.
2. Return to The Darknet District to collect payment.

Do not award payment immediately upon delivery.

Once delivery is confirmed, the cargo mission should update to the return-for-payment objective.

## Iris Replaces Admin

After the first faction delivery, Iris becomes the primary shop contact for these missions.

Admin does not need to be removed permanently from the game.

His temporary absence should not trigger a major storyline or explanation unless separately approved.

## Mission Availability

The faction mission the player did not originally select must remain available.

The player can accept it from Iris after collecting payment for the first delivery.

The player must not be forced to accept the second contract immediately.

## Faction Neutrality

Completing deliveries for either faction does not establish political allegiance.

The player is acting as an independent cargo pilot.

Neither delivery should automatically lock the player into a faction storyline.

## Mission Tracking

Each accepted delivery must appear in the Mission Log.

After the cargo reaches its destination, the tracked objective changes to:

"Return to The Darknet District for payment."

When the player accepts the second contract, its destination becomes the tracked objective.

## Save System

Save the following information:

- Which faction delivery was accepted first.
- Whether cargo was delivered.
- Whether payment was collected.
- Whether the remaining contract is available.
- Whether the second contract was accepted.
- Whether the second delivery was completed.
- Whether the second payment was collected.

Prevent duplicate payment rewards or repeated cargo creation.
