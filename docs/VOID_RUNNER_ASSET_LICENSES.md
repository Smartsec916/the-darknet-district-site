# VOID//RUNNER: Third-Party 3D Asset Licences

Every third-party model, texture or HDRI in the game is listed here before it ships.
- **Licence is pass/fail:** commercial use must be allowed.
- **Not allowed:** anything extracted from commercial games, and anything without a clear licence.

**Fields:**
- asset name, creator, source URL
- licence, attribution required (yes/no), modification and redistribution terms
- download date, local path, approval status
